# Municipal Water Operations — Professional Learning Record

**Current role:** Junior Water Operator  
**Organisation:** City of Calgary — Water Operations (DWD)

## Purpose
This is a public-safe professional development template. It is intended to capture general learning and transferable skills while respecting City policies, safety obligations, operational security and confidentiality.

## Learning themes
- High-level understanding of municipal water infrastructure.
- Operational discipline, safety culture and procedural awareness.
- Communication, teamwork and coordination in an essential-service environment.
- Understanding the relationship between field operations, maintenance, service reliability and public service.
- Continuous improvement and reflective learning.

## Do not record or publish here
- Non-public maps, facility or network layouts, asset identifiers or exact locations.
- SCADA, control-system, cybersecurity or access-control information.
- Operating setpoints, vulnerabilities, emergency response details or security procedures.
- Work orders, incident reports, customer details, staff information or internal communications.
- Photographs or documents unless explicitly authorised for public release.
