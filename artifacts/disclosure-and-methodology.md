# Data disclosure, professional integrity and methodology

This portfolio separates:
1. **Owner-described experience:** Roles, sectors and project types supplied by the portfolio owner. They are not independently audited by this website.
2. **Public-safe professional learning:** General reflections that contain no restricted operational or employer information.
3. **Synthetic demonstration artefacts:** Fictional sample data, charts, schedules and templates used to demonstrate analytical methods.

## City of Calgary / municipal water operations
The portfolio may identify the current role as Junior Water Operator, City of Calgary Water Operations (DWD), as supplied by the portfolio owner. It must not imply City endorsement or disclose internal operational information. Do not publish non-public network maps, facility layouts, asset identifiers, control-system details, operating parameters, vulnerabilities, emergency procedures, work orders, incident reports, customer or staff information, internal communications, or photographs/documents without explicit authorisation.

## Other named organisations
Do not use logos, internal screenshots, letterheads, signatures, patient data, employee data, supplier details, confidential budgets, contracts, system architecture, procurement information or commercially sensitive records without permission. If identity is not cleared for publication, use a general sector label.

## Metrics and proof
Actual project KPIs may be added only when supported by source records and cleared for disclosure. Synthetic data must remain visibly labelled as synthetic. Do not claim project savings, schedule recovery, completion percentages, contract values or outcomes without evidence.

## Evidence classification
- **Experience description:** Owner-provided summary, not independently verified.
- **Template:** Blank structure for future use.
- **Synthetic demonstration:** Fictional data for visualisation or learning.
- **Verified evidence:** A genuine record that the owner has permission to disclose, with sensitive fields redacted and provenance explained.
